Pashun Sports Customer Policy Pack

The Customer Policy Pack is three documents. They are stored once, in the shared Resources folder of this handout pack, not in this lecture folder:

1. Pashun Sports – US Returns and Exchanges Policy.docx
2. Pashun Sports – US Delivery Timescales.docx
3. Pashun Pro Tee – Sizing and Fabric FAQ.docx

Upload all three to the Customer Policies library on the Pashun Sports T-Shirts team site (https://pashunsports.sharepoint.com/sites/PashunSports), then add that library as the agent's knowledge source, together with the public page pashunsports.example/faq.

Do not add any other returns documents (for example the Operations Manual Extract): overlapping sources give overlapping answers.
