# Lecture 14: How to Design a Customer Service Agent Using Knowledge Grounding

**In this folder:** Customer-Policy-Pack-README.md, Customer-Service-Specialist-Instruction-Block.docx, Customer-Service-Test-Questions.docx

Customer-Policy-Pack-README.md points to the three Customer Policy Pack documents, which live in Resources/ under their exact names: `Resources/Pashun Sports – US Returns and Exchanges Policy.docx`, `Resources/Pashun Sports – US Delivery Timescales.docx` and `Resources/Pashun Pro Tee – Sizing and Fabric FAQ.docx`. Upload them to your Customer Policies library, use the Customer Service Specialist Instruction Block for the description and grounding rules, and then run the Customer Service Test Questions in the Test panel, checking every citation.

## To follow along exactly
Have the three policy documents in a SharePoint library (or a library you own) and your Release 1 Customer Service Agent open.
