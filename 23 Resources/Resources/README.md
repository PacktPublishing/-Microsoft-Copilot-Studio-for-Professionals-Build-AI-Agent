# Resources: shared sample data

These files are used by many lectures in Microsoft Copilot Studio: The Complete Guide (2026). Upload or import them as the Lecture 02 Setup Guide describes. Lecture numbers show where the script names or demos each file.

## Knowledge documents (upload to the Customer Policies library in SharePoint)
- `Pashun Sports – US Returns and Exchanges Policy.docx` - the single source of truth for returns, refund approval, exchanges, damaged items and non-returnable items (Sections 1-5). Lectures 7, 8, 14, 18, 22.
- `Pashun Pro Tee – Sizing and Fabric FAQ.docx` - size chart, fit, fabric, care and the personalized-print final-sale rule. Lectures 7, 8, 14, 22.
- `Pashun Sports – US Delivery Timescales.docx` - US-only delivery options, prices and cut-off times. Lectures 7, 13, 14.
- `Pashun Pro Tee – US Range Brief.docx` - product spec, colors, sizes and commercial terms. It is deliberately confidential and internal (wholesale and cost figures). Lectures 7, 8, 19, 24.
- `Pashun Sports – Operations Manual Extract.docx` - internal notes, including the 28-day wholesale return window that conflicts with the 30-day public window (the source-conflict test in Lecture 8).

## Lists and data
- `Pashun Sports – US Supplier Contacts.xlsx` (table USSupplierContacts) and `.csv` - import as the "US Supplier Contacts" SharePoint list. Lectures 2, 15, 19.
- `Pashun Sports – Returns Log (list schema and sample rows).xlsx` - columns and 8 sample rows for the "Returns Log" list (PS-1042 to PS-1049). Lecture 11, also Lectures 9, 12.
- `Pashun Sports – Inbox Log (list schema and sample rows).xlsx` - columns and sample rows for the "Inbox Log" list. Lecture 20.
- `Pashun Pro Tee – US Launch Plan (Planner tasks).xlsx` - 16 tasks for the Planner plan "Pashun Pro Tee US Launch", with a Read me sheet. Lectures 2, 15, 19, 25. Two tasks (Approve print artwork, Sample run QA) are due before the Volt Green fabric arrives on Nov 24, 2026, which is the risk the Launch Briefing agent finds in Lecture 19.

## Sample emails (Sample Emails folder, .eml; open in Outlook or send to yourself)
- `Ridgeline-01-Fabric-Quote-Request.eml`, `Ridgeline-02-Fabric-Quote-Reply.eml`, `Ridgeline-03-Delivery-Update.eml` - the "Fabric quote – Pashun Pro Tee" thread between Jamie and Casey Brooks. Lectures 2, 15, 19, 25.
- `Customer-Return-01.eml` to `Customer-Return-06.eml` - polite exchange (PS-1043), the messy swap email (PS-1042, Lecture 9/10), an angry late-delivery return (PS-1044), a damaged bulk order over $100 (PS-1046), a return outside the 30-day window (PS-1045) and a personalized print (PS-1047). Lectures 2, 9, 10, 11, 12, 14, 18.
- `Inbox-Test-01-Ridgeline-Delay.eml`, `Inbox-Test-02-Sizing-Question.eml`, `Inbox-Test-03-Refund-Request-150.eml`, `Inbox-Test-04-Newsletter.eml` - the four test emails for the Inbox Sort and Respond workflow. Lecture 20.
- `All-Sample-Emails.docx` - every email as readable text, for people who cannot open .eml files.
