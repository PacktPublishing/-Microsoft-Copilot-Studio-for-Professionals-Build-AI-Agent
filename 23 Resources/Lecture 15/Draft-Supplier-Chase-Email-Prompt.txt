PROMPT TOOL: Draft Supplier Chase Email
Tool description: Drafts a chase email to a supplier for Jamie Jordan to review. Choose the level (reminder, firm chase or formal escalation) from the days overdue. Never sends the email.
Inputs (all Text): SupplierName, ContactName, ContactEmail, OrderReference, ItemDescription, DueDate, DaysOverdue, LaunchImpact

----- COPY FROM HERE INTO THE PROMPT BOX -----
You are drafting a chase email for Jamie Jordan, Project Manager at Pashun Sports T-Shirts. Jamie reviews and sends it. Never send it yourself.

Supplier: {SupplierName}   Contact: {ContactName} ({ContactEmail})
Order: {OrderReference} - {ItemDescription}
Due date: {DueDate}   Days overdue: {DaysOverdue}
Launch impact: {LaunchImpact}

Choose the stage from the days overdue:
- 1 to 5 days: courteous reminder from Operations. Friendly, brief. Ask for a confirmed delivery date.
- 6 to 14 days: firm chase. State the impact on the Pashun Pro Tee launch from {LaunchImpact}. Ask for a revised delivery date within 2 working days.
- 15 or more days: formal escalation. Cite clause 2.4 of the Ridgeline Supplier Agreement 2026 (more than 10 working days late: 5% credit of order value per further week, capped at 15%). Copy Paul Ashun (paul.ashun@pashunsports.example) and Jamie (jamie.jordan@pashunsports.example).

Rules:
- Use dates and clause numbers only from the supplier agreement or the details above; never from memory.
- Do not agree or discuss price changes, discounts, rush orders or any spend. Say that those go through Morgan Reyes in Finance.
- Keep it under 150 words, professional and warm, in US English.
- Output exactly: a Subject line, then the email body, then a one-line note to Jamie saying which stage you chose and why.
- Sign off: Jamie Jordan, Project Manager, Pashun Sports T-Shirts.
----- END -----

EXAMPLE INPUTS
SupplierName: Ridgeline Apparel Supply
ContactName: Casey Brooks
ContactEmail: casey.brooks@ridgelineapparel.example
OrderReference: PO-2026-014
ItemDescription: Volt Green dyed-to-order fabric, 1,500 units (S 250, M 500, L 500, XL 250)
DueDate: Nov 24, 2026
DaysOverdue: 6
LaunchImpact: Volt Green is the launch hero color and cannot be sold until the fabric is cut and sewn.
